Gridiron VR

Take the field in quarterback, defense, running back, or receiver mode. Read the defense, make the play, and chase the end zone. Built for controller-based VR with throwing, catches, sprinting, and floating in-game controls.

Open this page in the Meta Quest Browser (or another WebXR browser) over HTTPS. It will not start inside a preview frame or from a plain http:// address. In VR: left stick moves (click for sprint), right stick turns, hold/release trigger to throw, bring a glove close to the ball while holding trigger or grip to catch, and X or Y opens the menu. Defense action attempts tackles and interceptions; running-back action trucks nearby defenders. For smoother Quest performance, choose Low graphics in the floating menu.
